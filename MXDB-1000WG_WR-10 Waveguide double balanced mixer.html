General Description

MXDB-1000WG is a double balanced mixer that features excellent conversion loss, great isolations, and spurious performance across the bandwidth. The MXDB-1000WG works well as both an up and down converter. The MXDB-1000WG is recommended for WR-10 frequency band conversion applications that require high linearity.

Features

  • Full band, high linearity mmWave frequency conversion
  • Up or down conversion
  • UG-387/U flanges, RF and LO ports
  • 1.85mm Female IF port connector
